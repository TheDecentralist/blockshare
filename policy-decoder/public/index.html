STEP 07 LTE Lifetime Efficiency Index — Jurisdiction Comparator
The Lifetime Time Efficiency (LTE) Index measures what fraction of a person's waking lifetime is consumed obtaining basic necessities — housing, transit, food, healthcare, childcare. Lower score = more lifetime returned to residents. This policy is scored against three benchmark jurisdictions that represent different governance models and LTE outcomes.
