00 · Architecture

The Community Economy Tool Stack

Five distinct layers — each one a door into the next. Understanding the stack prevents confusion at the table and makes it clear what to hand someone at each stage of engagement.

Signal Layer
🏷 The Price Tag — Symbol & Signal
A visual icon, not a form factor. On Shoppe items it signals dual pricing ($ + CC). On merchant doors it means "Alt Payments Accepted Here." Also the workshop / talk / book chapter title.
↓
Action Layer
📋 Quest Card — Onboarding & Action Tool
Recipe card format (~4"×6"). Numbered, audience-matched. Converts curiosity into action. Completion requires registration + vouching — building the trust graph organically.
↓
Identity & Payment Layer
💳 Community Card — The New Economy Credential
Multi-edition, demographic-segmented. Familiar card form factor — just pointing in a different direction. Active functions live now; pending functions shown in the UI to signal the emerging system.
↓
Currency Layer
⬡ Community Credyts — Medium of Exchange
Earned via Quests, purchased at the Shoppe, or received as seigniorage. Loaded onto the Community Card. Backed 33/33/33: Cash / Labour / Assets.
↓
Ecosystem Layer
🏘 Block Share — The Platform
The full neighbourhood resource-sharing, micro-asset, and governance platform. Grows as participants join, merchants sign on, and Quests are completed.

03 · Community Card Suite

Card Editions — Launch & Next Wave

The Community Card is the primary identity and payment credential of the Community Economy. It is a table product in its own right — familiar enough to pick up without explanation, different enough to start a conversation. Active functions are live on day one; pending functions are shown in the UI to signal an emerging system to early adopters.

Early Adopter framing: Pending functions shown greyed-out in the card UI are not gaps — they are the roadmap. Early adopters are not missing features; they are part of building the system. Being among the first 100 cardholders is a credential in itself.

✦ Launch Edition

Visitor+

Transport, local food bonuses, entertainment, events & attractions. Transferable. Perfect Granville Island intercept for visitors.

05 · Rollout

Phase-by-Phase Implementation

Phase 0 · Prep

Guerrilla Assembly — Start With What You Have

  • Borrow tables & chairs (or sponsor); DIY or sponsored sandwich board with 🏷 symbol
  • Print Quest Cards (recipe format) and Community Cards (4 launch editions)
  • Every item — including every card — gets a dual-price hangtag before the table opens
  • Sign-up form live; myCredyts payment rails confirmed; receiving bank account set up
  • 3–5 Granville Island merchants confirmed with QR codes + 🏷 door stickers
Phase 1 · First Intercept

The Demo Table Goes Live

  • Deploy at Granville Island — foot traffic, independent merchants, contained pilot geography
  • Pay What You Can / Need model — the table IS the demonstration of the alt economy
  • "Price Tag" (Jessie J) as ambient audio hook (permission sourced or alt version used)
  • Host matches Quest Card + Community Card edition to each visitor's audience type
  • First Credyts loaded on Quest completion; vouching mechanic begins
Phase 2 · Talk Track

"The Price is NOT Right" as Education

  • 45–60 min workshop / talk — available to schools, organisations, community event organisers
  • Frames the "Real Price Tag" → Life Accounting / Account model → LEI → FEI
  • Quest: "Calculate Your LTE" is the take-home action; Community Card is the take-home tool
  • The Shoppe can run as an "alternative table" at any gathering — no advocacy required from the host
Phase 3 · Network Weave

Cards + Quests Build the Social Graph

  • Each completed Quest + vouch = one new verified node in the Block Share network
  • Community Card holders spend Credyts at participating merchants; merchant QR codes are the spending rail
  • 🏷 symbol visible on merchant doors — the signal network becomes a neighbourhood phenomenon
  • Local groups (schools, clubs, causes) fund projects through Community Card pre-sales
Phase 4 · Scale

Pending Functions Activate — The Suite Comes Online

  • Transit pass integration, WiFi hotspot access, Global Human Passport link
  • Ecological sub-wallets go live (CO₂ / O₂ / Farmland per card)
  • Prize draws activate: Win your Rent, Win your Groceries (daily / weekly / monthly)
  • Block Edition cards issued as neighbourhood density reaches threshold
  • myCredyts multi-capital sub-wallets: #Tag + Value accounting across all card types

Every element is both a standalone thing and a door into the next. The system builds itself as people participate. Early adopters are not joining a finished product — they are building it by being here.
