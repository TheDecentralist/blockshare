01 · About

The Body of Work in Brief

Ward operates at the intersection of community organizing, platform design, and alternative economics. Where most critics stop at diagnosis, Ward builds tools. His frameworks have been applied to Vancouver neighbourhood budgeting, housing policy reform, complementary currency systems, and ecological stewardship — producing a cohesive body of work that is simultaneously theoretical and immediately deployable.

His foundational thinking was first published in 2020 under the byline The Decentralist on Medium — three essays establishing the core argument: that the power to create money has been surrendered to private corporations, that a community-controlled alternative is both historically precedented and practically achievable, and that the hierarchical extraction systems underpinning modern capitalism are a continuation of empire by other means.

His flagship platform, Block Share (app.blockshare.ca), is a live proof-of-concept for what a community-owned micro-economy looks like in practice. His policy publications apply the LEI/FEI framework to Vancouver housing with forensic precision. His forthcoming book Default provides the narrative and intellectual scaffolding for the entire project.

"The economy isn't failing — it's working exactly as designed, but for the wrong people. Ward is resequencing the Economic DNA to work for All of Us, and for Life."
Every city block already has a multi-million dollar economy flowing through it. Nobody accounts for it. Blockonomics makes that invisible power visible.
02 · Original Frameworks & Systems

Creator Of

LEI — Lifetime Efficiency Index
A replacement for GDP that measures how much of a person's life is spent working to meet basic needs. Paired with the Financial Extraction Index (FEI), it exposes the true cost of rentier capitalism in lived hours.
Community Credyts
A complementary currency with demurrage, seigniorage, and tripartite backing (Cash / Labour / Assets). Designed to reduce fiat dependency and keep value circulating locally.
Block Share Platform
A neighbourhood resource-sharing and micro-asset platform. The civic infrastructure layer that transforms a city block into a cooperative economy. Live at app.blockshare.ca.
10 Houses Architecture
A stewardship framework organising bioregional community life into ten relational houses — the ecological and social infrastructure for a post-extractive neighbourhood.
Mobility Multi-Pass
A universal mobility access framework linking any NFC card to a TransLink account, settled via MYCC/LOCC blockchain tokens — making transit access community-owned.
Mobility for Life Programme
A policy framework for ending car dependency at household and municipal scale — combining lifetime cost analysis, transit-first infrastructure, and the Mobility Multi-Pass universal access system.
Art of Money
A participatory currency literacy initiative exploring what money is, who makes it, and what it could be — grounded in community storytelling and experiential learning.
Life Hours — Universal Currency
Time-backed currency equivalent that denominates economic exchange in units of human life — a radical reframing of value that makes exploitation visible.
City Cards — Universal Pass & Agent
Locally-owned alternatives to Amazon and Uber: community-governed platforms that keep platform economics inside the neighbourhood rather than extracting them to shareholders.
Personal Biosphere Dashboard & Wallet
An integrated Global Human Passport and ecological accounting tool — tracking individual stewardship, carbon, and multi-capital wealth in a single personal dashboard.
Micro-Assets System
A framework for community members to own fractional shares in the productive assets of their neighbourhood — the infrastructure layer beneath Community Credyts.
The 3rd Way
A political-economic framework charting a new path between Markets and Commons — the theoretical spine connecting all of Ward's platform work.
Food for Generations — K–12 Programme
A school-based food sovereignty programme connecting every student to 12 m² of farmland. Class Farms, polyculture food forests, and a full K–12 curriculum designed to embed ecological literacy and community food systems from age five.

06 · Games & Educational Tools

Learn by Playing

Ward designs interactive games and workshop formats that make complex systems viscerally understandable. Each game encodes real economic data — players don't just learn about extraction, they feel it happening.

House of Cars — 54-Card Street Game
A pocket-sized card game that makes the $2 million lifetime cost of car dependency personal. Five suits — Car, Transit, Bike, Walk, and Joker (wildcards) — built from real Canadian cost data. Designed for students, policy makers, community evangelists, and the general public. Companion to the Mobility for Life policy paper and podcast.
Game of Towers
An educational simulation of the power dynamics inside economic and governance systems — designed for civic engagement and community literacy. Players build, trade, and discover how rules shape outcomes.
The Price Isn't Right — Pop-Up Shoppe
A street-level community economics workshop disguised as a market stall. Participants discover the hidden extraction embedded in everyday prices — rent, interest, platform fees, and profit margins that never appear on a receipt.
