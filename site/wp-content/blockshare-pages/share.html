How It Works

The Block Share Difference

Not a marketplace. A neighbourhood sharing system.

📍

Hyper-Local

Everything happens within your building or block. Walk to borrow, walk to return.

🤝

Host-Managed

Your Block Host coordinates loans, manages pool inventory, and resolves any issues.

◆

Credyt-Powered

No cash between neighbours. Community Credyts track contributions and access.

🌱

Ecologically Sound

Less manufacturing, less shipping, less waste. Every shared item is one less produced.
