What Are Community Credits?

Community Credits (CC) are Block Share's points program — a complementary currency that circulates within your neighbourhood. You earn CC by participating in community activities: completing surveys, sharing items, hosting food deliveries, volunteering time.

Unlike loyalty points at a corporation, CC are backed by real community assets and designed to strengthen local economic connections. They're the beginning of a neighbourhood-scale economy that keeps value where it's created.

Think of CC as a community loyalty program today — evolving toward a full complementary currency as the network grows. Every credit you earn is a vote for your neighbourhood's economic independence.

The Tri-Partite Backing Model

Every Community Credit in circulation is backed by three sources of real community value — not by speculation, not by debt, but by the actual productive capacity of your neighbourhood.

33%

Cash Reserve

A portion of every CC issued is backed by cash held in a community reserve fund. This provides a floor value and enables CC-to-dollar redemption.

• Direct cash contributions • Revenue from Beef Collective margins • Local business partner deposits
33%

Labour Commitment

Members pledge hours of skilled labour — anything from tutoring to carpentry to gardening. These pledges back CC with real productive capacity.

• Professional services at community rates • Block maintenance and improvements • Skills-sharing and workshops
33%

Asset Value

Shared tools, equipment, food inventory, and eventually community-held property back CC with tangible asset value.

• Sharing pool inventory value • Beef Collective inventory • Future: Permanent Land Trust equity

Why CC Has Demurrage

Community Credits lose 2% of their idle value each month. This isn't a penalty — it's a design feature borrowed from some of the world's most successful community currencies.

Demurrage encourages circulation. When CC moves — from person to person, from neighbour to local shop — it multiplies its impact. Hoarded currency helps no one. Circulating currency builds a neighbourhood economy.

Think of it as the opposite of interest: instead of rewarding those who sit on money, demurrage rewards those who use it. This keeps CC flowing and prevents accumulation by a few.

Tip: Spend or invest your CC regularly. Lock CC into block projects or community assets to avoid demurrage — invested CC holds its full value.
